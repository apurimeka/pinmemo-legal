Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy describes how PinMemo ("the App") handles your information.

1. Information stored by the App

Memos you create — their text, creation date/time, and reminder date/time — are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer's servers. There are no accounts to create.

2. Notifications

The App uses local notifications on your device to pin memos and deliver reminders. Notification content is never sent outside your device.

3. Advertising

The free version shows rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. In connection with ad delivery, Google may collect information such as the advertising identifier (IDFA). For details, please see Google's policies.

Your memo text is never sent to advertisers.

The Pro version shows no ads.

4. In-App Purchases

Pro purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. The developer never receives your payment information, such as credit card numbers.

5. Analytics

The App does not use any third-party analytics tools.

6. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.
